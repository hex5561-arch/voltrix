Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026 | Effective Date: October 13, 2026

1. Overview and Purpose

Voltrix OS ("Voltrix", "we", "us", or "our"), available at https://voltrix.stream, is a distraction-free academic operating system engineered for students, educators, and academic researchers. Voltrix provides academic curriculum workspaces, Socratic AI tutoring, and an educational video theater (V-Tube).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our platform, with specific emphasis on our integration with YouTube API Services and Google OAuth.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information strictly required to deliver academic learning tools:

  • Account Information: When you register or authenticate, we collect your display name, username, and email address.
  • Academic Profile Data: Enrolled courses, chosen discipline (e.g., Computer Science, Mathematics), academic year, institution name, and self-identified learning objectives.
  • Google and YouTube Data (Voluntary Integration): If you choose to connect your Google account to enable live YouTube synchronization in V-Tube, we receive an OAuth 2.0 access token and refresh token granted under your explicit consent for the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl scope. We do not access your private emails, Google Drive files, personal search history, or personal YouTube watch history.

3. How We Use YouTube API Services

Voltrix OS uses YouTube API Services to provide students with distraction-free educational lecture viewing. By using these features, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Specifically, our application requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl scope for the following explicit educational actions initiated solely by you:

  • Liking Educational Videos: When you click the "Like" button on an academic lecture in V-Tube, the YouTube Data API (videos.rate) is called on your behalf to record your positive rating directly to YouTube.
  • Subscribing to Academic Channels: When you click the "Subscribe" button on a lecture creator or university channel (e.g., MIT OpenCourseWare, 3Blue1Brown, Stanford Online), the YouTube Data API (subscriptions.insert or subscriptions.delete) is called on your behalf to manage that subscription on your YouTube account.
🛡️ Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Voltrix's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We only use Google/YouTube data to provide user-facing features (liking lectures and subscribing to channels at your instruction).
  • We do NOT transfer or sell Google user data to third parties, advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers.
  • We do NOT use Google user data for serving advertisements, including personalized, re-targeted, or interest-based advertising.
  • We do NOT use Google user data to train generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.

4. Data Storage, Security, and Token Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (including HTTPS/TLS in transit and encrypted key storage) to safeguard your data.

  • OAuth access tokens are used in real-time to execute authorized API calls and are stored securely in browser storage or encrypted sessions.
  • Tokens are never exposed in public repositories, client-facing logs, or unauthenticated endpoints.
  • Tokens are retained only as long as necessary to maintain your connected session or until you choose to disconnect.

5. How to Revoke Access and Delete Data

You have complete control over your Google and YouTube data at all times:

  • Within Voltrix: You can disconnect your YouTube account at any time by clicking the "Disconnect YouTube" option in your V-Tube theater dashboard. This instantly clears your access tokens from local storage.
  • Via Google Security Settings: You can revoke Voltrix's access to your Google account at any time by visiting the official Google Account Third-Party Permissions page and clicking "Remove Access".
  • Account Deletion: You can request full deletion of your Voltrix account and all associated profile data by emailing our support team at emmanuelvoltrix24@gmail.com. All associated data will be permanently purged within 30 days.

6. Third-Party Services and Links

Voltrix OS contains links to external educational content hosted on YouTube and university servers. We encourage you to review the privacy notices of all third-party sites you visit:

  • YouTube Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Account Permissions Manager

7. Children's Privacy

Voltrix OS is designed for university students, educators, and adult researchers. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.
